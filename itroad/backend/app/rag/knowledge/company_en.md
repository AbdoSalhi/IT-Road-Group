## About ITROAD Group
ITROAD Group builds technology that moves enterprises forward. Since 2009, ITROAD Group has been a trusted partner for digital transformation across the Middle East and Africa.

## Our mission
To empower organizations with innovative technology solutions that create lasting value, drive efficiency, and unlock new possibilities for growth.

## Our vision
To be the region's most trusted digital transformation partner, recognized for technical excellence, innovation, and the measurable impact we create for every client.

## Company track record
ITROAD Group has 15+ years of expertise, has delivered 250+ projects, works with 60+ enterprise clients, and has a 98% client retention rate.

## Company history
ITROAD Group was founded in 2009 with a vision for enterprise digital transformation. In 2013 it expanded regionally, opening offices across North Africa and the Middle East. In 2017 it launched dedicated cloud and artificial intelligence practices. In 2020 it achieved top-tier partnerships with Microsoft, AWS, and Google Cloud. By 2024 it had surpassed 250 enterprise projects delivered across 6 industries.

## Our values
ITROAD Group is guided by four values: Integrity (building trust through transparency and accountability), Innovation (pushing boundaries and embracing emerging technologies), Client-Centric approach (the client's success defines ours, every decision starts with the client), and Excellence (holding ourselves to the highest standards in everything we deliver).
